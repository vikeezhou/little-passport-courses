Little Passport course revision 2: en-listen-1 version 2.
The retained Piper Cori PCM originally contained unintended speech after the initial word and a long silence. Only the original samples [0,10400) at 16000 Hz are retained (650 ms); the complete initial word and trailing quiet are preserved. Original and trimmed hashes, sample range and source identity are recorded in red-source.json and audio-source-manifest.json. No source PCM, voice models, engine, user recordings or device data are published.
The v1 package is retained unchanged for historical firmware. The root manifest remains revision 1 until matching firmware is ready. A revision-1 firmware catalog rejects a revision-2 root manifest; update the matching firmware before download acceptance. Original SOURCE.txt, voice model cards, LICENSE.txt and PUBLICATION.txt remain applicable.
